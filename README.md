Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty "The Absurd Clause Contest": one line, 200 characters at most — an absurd clause hidden deep inside a terms-of-service agreement.

## Entry

`main.py` (Python standard library only, deterministic) writes a single original English sentence to `/output/entry.txt`:

> By accepting these terms, you grant us a perpetual, irrevocable licence to your soul, including any souls you acquire later, which we may sublicense to our affiliates, subsidiaries, and the Moon.

195 characters, one line. Deadpan legal boilerplate that quietly turns absurd.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
